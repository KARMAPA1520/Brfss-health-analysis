# The Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS) 
is the nation’s premier system of health-related telephone surveys that collect state data about US residents regarding their health-related risk behaviors, chronic health conditions, health-care access, and use of preventive services. Established in 1984, BRFSS collects data in all 50 states as well as the District of Columbia and participating US territories. BRFSS completes more than 400,000 adult interviews each year, making it the largest continuously conducted telephone-based health survey system in the world.
